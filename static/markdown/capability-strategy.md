2.  Capabilities
    x.  Capabilities are used to produce goods and services. A good or service is a reflection of a bundle of capabilities.
    a.  Capability = Asset applied to activity
    b.  Assets (noun): Physical, human, informational, organizational
    c.  Activities (verb): product-focused, asset-focused
    d.  Many-to-many asset-activity cardinality
    e.  Level of generality (individual, team, etc.)
    f.  Can exist within a firm or be leveraged through strategic partners.
